What Makes Us Different

01

Fair by default

Weavers set their own price for every piece and are paid in full on delivery — not 60 or 90 days later like most wholesale arrangements. No piece leaves the loom until the weaver has already been paid for it.

02

Real handloom

Every saree carries a loom ID tied to the weaver who made it. We don't carry power-loom copies dressed up as handloom — if a piece can't be traced to a loom and a name, it doesn't enter the catalogue.

03

Made to last

Natural dyes are tested for colourfastness before a single saree ships. First orders come with a free re-fall and pico, because a saree that doesn't hang right doesn't get worn.

From the founder

Every label has
a name on it.

My grandfather wove Jamdani on a pit loom two streets from where our workshop stands today. When we started Joba, the one rule we didn't compromise on was this: if you buy a saree from us, you can look up exactly who made it. That hasn't changed, and it isn't going to.
